# Jessi Ray Confusion Tag Sheet

Assign every useful feedback answer to one primary tag.

## Tag list
1. `Did not understand what KandyDrops is`
   - The fan did not understand the product purpose

2. `Did not know what to click first`
   - The fan did not know the first action after signup

3. `Did not understand GumDrops`
   - The fan did not understand currency, balance, or why they needed it

4. `Wanted to talk but did not understand message cost`
   - The fan expected messaging to work differently or did not understand the first-message path

5. `Wanted content but did not understand unwrap`
   - The fan did not understand what unwrap means or when to use it

6. `Did not trust signup`
   - The fan hesitated because of trust, account creation, or platform legitimacy

7. `Hit a real bug`
   - The fan was blocked by a broken screen, missing control, failed action, or loading issue

8. `Too many steps`
   - The fan understood the product but felt the process was too long

## Logging rule
- One primary tag per user answer
- Add a short note only if needed
- If the answer points to a real bug, include the exact step and route them to in-site Support

## Example tagging
`I made the account but didn't realize I had to follow you first`
- Tag: `Did not know what to click first`

`I thought unwrap meant a gift box or something`
- Tag: `Wanted content but did not understand unwrap`

`The message button kept failing`
- Tag: `Hit a real bug`

`I didn't want to put my info into another site`
- Tag: `Did not trust signup`
