# Jessi Ray Feedback Prompt Card

Use this only after the fan has done one meaningful action:
- signed up and followed
- sent the first message
- tried to unwrap
- tried to request or book

Do not ask for feedback before action.

## Exact questions
Ask these in plain language:

1. `What did you come here to get from me today?`
2. `What part was confusing or took too long?`
3. `What did you expect to happen next?`

If they say nothing was confusing:

4. `What almost stopped you from signing up?`

## When to escalate
Keep normal feedback in creator chat.

Only route to support if they are actually blocked by:
- signup failure
- missing page or button
- payment failure
- broken message, request, booking, or unwrap flow

Support handoff copy:

`Open Support in your dashboard and send: I was trying to [goal] and got stuck at [step].`

## What counts as a useful answer
Useful:
- "I didn't know I had to follow first."
- "I thought message was free forever."
- "I didn't understand what unwrap meant."
- "I didn't trust the signup page."
- "I couldn't tell which button to hit."

Not useful:
- "Looks good"
- "idk"

If the answer is too vague, ask one follow-up:

`What exact step felt weird or confusing?`
