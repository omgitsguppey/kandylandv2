## One universal walkthrough
Use this when they say yes and need next steps:

1. Click my KandyDrops link.
2. Make your free account.
3. Hit **Follow** on my page.
4. Hit the action you came for:
   - **Message** if you want to talk
   - **Custom request** if you want something made for you
   - **Call or video** if you want time with me
   - **Unwrap** if you want the drop or content
5. Use your free GumDrops to start.
6. Check in daily if you want more free GumDrops.

## Objection handling
Keep every reply to one sentence and one next step.

### "I don't want another app or site"
`You only need a free account so I can actually respond there without social media getting in the way, so click my KandyDrops link and follow me there.`

### "Is it free?"
`Yes, the account is free, and you start with free GumDrops so you can get going, so make the account and follow me first.`

### "I don't know what to do after I sign up"
`Just follow me first, then tap message if you want to talk, or tap the exact thing you came for.`

### "I don't want to pay"
`You don't need to start by paying, just make the free account, use your free GumDrops, and check in daily if you want more.`

### "I'm confused"
`Tell me exactly what button confused you, and I'll walk you through it.`

## What not to say
- Do not pitch referral as the main incentive
- Do not pitch "the community" before the fan's ask
- Do not give multiple CTAs in one reply
- Do not explain GumDrops unless the fan reaches a gated step

## Default closing line
`Make the free account, follow me, and hit the exact thing you came for.`
