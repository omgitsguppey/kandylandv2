# KandyDrops Copy Doctrine

## Purpose
This document establishes the detailed copy rules for all surfaces across KandyDrops. It dictates tone, vocabulary, and acceptable rhetoric.

---

## 1. Tone by Surface

### Core User Surfaces
* **Landing Page:** Aspirational, high-value, and focused on access. Minimal friction.
* **Wallet:** Precise, trustworthy, and transactional. No fluff.
* **Drops / Experiences Tab:** Flirty, appetite-inducing, premium. Focus on scarcity and exclusivity.
* **Preview Modal / Viewer:** Focused, immersive, and clear about the value being unlocked.
* **Creator Profiles:** Intimate, authentic, and focused on the connection between creator and fan.
* **Notifications:** Brief, anticipated, and highly relevant. Never spammy.

### Operational Surfaces
* **Support / Settings:** Calm, grouped, scannable. (iOS-style). Clear directions, no personality-heavy jokes.
* **Admin / Dashboard:** Brutally truthful. Operational clarity over marketing. If it's broken, say it's broken.

---

## 2. Density and Structure
* **Density:** Rely on high-density, scannable blocks.
* **Hierarchy:** Never duplicate meaning between a Title and its Subtext. 
    * *Title:* What is this?
    * *Subtext:* Why do I care / What is the detail?
* **Brevity:** If copy is too vague, too long, or too robotic, rewrite it. Teach through structure, not paragraphs.

---

## 3. Call-to-Action (CTA) Writing Rules
* **Action-Oriented:** Start with a strong verb.
* **Contextual Truth:** CTAs must switch truthfully based on the user's state (e.g., if they cannot afford it, the CTA must reflect the required action, not just "Buy").
* **Forbidden:** Freestyled or generic CTAs when a specific action is required.

---

## 4. Urgency and Social Proof
* **Subtle Urgency:** Use urgency to encourage action, but avoid tacky panic (e.g., no "HURRY BEFORE IT'S GONE FOREVER!!!").
* **Earned Proof:** Social proof must be real and contextualized.

---

## 5. Approved Vocabulary & Rhetoric
* Use **GumDrops** for the economy.
* Use **Drops** or **Experiences** for content.
* Use **Creator** or **Fan**.

## 6. Disallowed Phrases / Anti-Patterns
* **Forbidden Words:** `Oops`, `Tokens`, `Credits`, `Points`.
* **Forbidden Rhetoric:** "It looks like..." (State the truth directly).
* **Robotic Tone:** "System error 404 occurred." -> Make it human but precise.
* **Over-Hype:** "The most amazing feature ever!" -> Let the product prove its value.
