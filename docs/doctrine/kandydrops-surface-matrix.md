# KandyDrops Surface Matrix

This matrix defines the primary job, emotional role, tone family, and allowed urgency for every major surface in the app. **Consult this before making changes to any specific page.**

| Surface | Primary Job | Emotional Role | Tone Family | CTA Rules | Allowed Urgency |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Homepage Hero** | Convert visitor to user/fan | Aspirational, welcoming | Premium, Accessible | Clear, low friction ("Explore", "Join") | Low |
| **Creator Spotlight** | Utility for growth, highlight top talent | Admiration, curiosity | Intimate, Premium | Direct to profile | Low-Medium (if event tied) |
| **How It Works** | Teach the system quickly | Clarity, trust | Calm, Informative | "Get Started" | None |
| **Drops Page** | Showcase available content | Appetite, desire | Flirty, Premium | Action-oriented | Medium (Scarcity) |
| **Featured Drop** | Drive immediate conversion | High desire | High Value | Value-based | High (Time/Supply limits) |
| **Locked Preview** | Tease value, explain unlock | Tease, anticipation | Flirty, Direct | Explicit cost ("Unlock for X GumDrops") | Medium |
| **Wallet** | Manage GumDrops, transactions | Security, truth | Transactional, Precise | "Refill", "Withdraw" | None |
| **My KandyDrops** | Collection management | Pride, ownership | Personal, Clean | "View", "Share" | None |
| **Viewer** | Consume content seamlessly | Immersion | Invisible (UI gets out of way) | Minimal | None |
| **Chat** | Direct connection with creator | Intimacy, exclusivity | Personal | "Send" | None |
| **Notifications** | Alert to high-value events | Anticipation | Brief, Urgent | Direct link | Medium |
| **Settings** | Manage preferences | Control, calm | iOS-style grouping | "Save", "Edit" | None |
| **Creator Profile** | Showcase creator brand & drops | Connection | Intimate | "Follow", "Unlock" | Low |
| **Creator Dashboard** | Business operational hub | Control, insight | Brutally truthful | "Create Drop", "Withdraw" | Low |
| **Admin Overview** | System health at a glance | Truth, clarity | Operational | "View Logs", "Manage" | High (if system unhealthy) |
| **Admin Analytics** | Drill down into data | Insight | Precise | N/A | None |

## What NOT to do:
* Do not apply "Featured Drop" urgency to the "Wallet" page.
* Do not make "Settings" flirty.
* Do not make "Creator Profiles" read like sterile Admin dashboards.
